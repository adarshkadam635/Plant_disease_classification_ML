SET YOUR SCAN UP FOR SUCCESS

A good photo makes a difference.

A few simple steps for a clearer view of your plant's health.

01

Find the light

Use soft, natural daylight. Avoid flash, harsh shadows, and bright reflections that hide details.

02

Focus on one leaf

Fill most of the frame with a single leaf. Keep the whole leaf visible and the surface sharp.

03

Keep it simple

Use a plain background and photograph the affected side. Upload JPEG, PNG, or WebP images under 10 MB.

04

Read with context

The model only knows the plants and conditions in the library. It can misclassify other plants and non-leaf images. Confidence is not diagnostic certainty.

Camera capture requires localhost or HTTPS and your browser's camera permission. If unavailable, use the device camera option or upload a photo.
